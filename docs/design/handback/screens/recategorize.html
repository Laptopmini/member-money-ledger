Recategorize

Picker → optimistic save → success or rollback, plus rule prompt, stale edit, disabled and AI suggestion.

1 · Picker

The Uncategorized chip is the picker: a native <select> styled as a chip (OS picker on mobile, full keyboard support, type-ahead). Options are grouped Business expenses / Not business / Money in; Income is disabled for money out.

2 · Optimistic update

The chip shows the new category at once with a small saving ring; the row flashes softly (200 ms). Summary, nudge and set-aside recompute immediately from local state.

3 · Saved

Quiet success toast, polite, auto-dismisses after 4 s.

4 · Merchant-rule prompt (flag: merchant-rules)

Inline under the row, only when other uncategorized rows from that merchant exist. Count is illustrative: the seed has no other Delta rows, so production would not show it here.

5 · Save failed: rolled back

The chip snaps back to Uncategorized (small shake, off under reduced motion) and an assertive toast offers Retry. The summary rolls back too.

6 · Stale edit (409)

Row refreshes to the new bank data, keeps Uncategorized, and explains what changed. No toast: the message belongs to the row.

7 · Disabled while vendor is down

Pickers disabled and aria-describedby the reason (also stated in the banner).

8 · AI suggestion chip (flag: category-suggestions)

Only for uncategorized rows with an unseen merchant. Plain icon-free chip; never auto-applied. “Thinking…” shimmer for at most 1 s, then hidden if nothing came back. Errors hide it silently.
